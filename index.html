Content Studio

A private tool for one creator's own gameplay videos

Content Studio is a private tool that one person, its owner, runs on the owner's own computer. It turns the owner's own PlayStation 5 gameplay recordings into short vertical videos (Shorts and Reels) and full mission videos, writes their titles and descriptions with the help of free AI writing services, and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Page, Instagram account and the other accounts the owner connects.

It is not a public service. Only the owner can sign in, it has no other users, and it acts only on the owner's own accounts.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads the owner's videos to the owner's channels and sets their thumbnails.
  • Keeps one playlist per game with the owner's mission videos, in story order.
  • Reads the owner's channel name and ID, to find the owner's own channel.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics (YouTube Analytics) to show charts to the owner.

Content Studio uses YouTube API Services.

Delete your data

Content Studio - last updated 25 November 2026

Content Studio keeps data only on its owner's own computer, and only about the owner's own accounts. To delete it:

  1. In Content Studio: open Accounts and press Disconnect on the account. This deletes the stored sign-in at once (for YouTube, also the stored YouTube statistics).
  2. Facebook and Instagram: on Facebook open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations), choose Content Studio and press Remove.
  3. Google and YouTube: remove Content Studio on the Google security settings page.
  4. Everything at once: deleting Content Studio's folder from the owner's computer deletes all it stored.

To ask for deletion, open an issue at github.com/rotomakeraki/rotomakeraki.github.io/issues; the data is deleted within 30 days.
